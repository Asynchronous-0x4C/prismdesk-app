# Code of Conduct

Issues and Discussions here are open to anyone, so this repository needs a
written answer to "what happens if someone behaves badly". This is it. It is
short on purpose: a longer document would not change what one person can
actually enforce.

## The short version

**Be someone the next person is glad to find in the thread.**

## The longer version

- **Argue with the problem, not the person.** Disagreeing about how PrismDesk
  should work is welcome and useful. Contempt for whoever is on the other end of
  it is not.
- **Assume the other person is doing their best with what they know.** Most
  reports that look careless are someone who does not yet know what to include.
- **No harassment, no slurs, no sexual attention, no personal attacks.**
- **Do not post anyone's private information** — theirs or your own. Order
  numbers, licence keys, email addresses, IP addresses and unread diagnostics
  files belong in an email, not in a public thread.
- **Off-topic is fine in Discussions and not in Issues.**

This applies in issues, in discussions, in release comments, and in any email
that starts from one of them.

## If something goes wrong

Email [support@prismdesk.app](mailto:support@prismdesk.app) and say what happened and
where. **PrismDesk is maintained by one person**, who reads it and decides —
there is no committee, no appeal process, and no promise on this page about how
fast.

What can follow: the comment is edited or removed, the thread is locked, or the
account is blocked from this repository. Usually in that order, and not always.

Reporting something in good faith never counts against you, even when the answer
turns out to be that nothing was wrong.
